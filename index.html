Our Philosophy

The table as a meeting point

For centuries, the caravanserais between Samarkand and the Fergana Valley fed travelers who shared nothing but the road. Iqbol keeps that principle: one long dastarkhan, dishes passed rather than plated in isolation, hospitality treated as the main course rather than a courtesy.

We cook with the same spices that once moved along the Silk Road — saffron, black cumin, barberry, sumac — but plate them with the restraint of a modern kitchen trained between Tashkent and Lyon.

Meet the Chef

"A recipe is a route. Change the destination and you lose the reason for the journey."

Iqbol Rahimov
Founder & Executive Chef

Trained in his grandmother's kitchen in the old city of Tashkent and later at Institut Paul Bocuse in Lyon, Chef Iqbol built this restaurant to hold both training at once — the discipline of a French pass and the generosity of a family dastarkhan. Every dish on the menu began as a recipe passed down before it was ever written.
